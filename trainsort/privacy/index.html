Train Sort Privacy Policy

Mastazee Studios
Last updated: 3 October 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Mastazee Studios ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use Train Sort ("the Game").

Train Sort is a mobile puzzle game developed and published by Mastazee Studios. The Game does not require users to create an account or provide a name, password, or profile.

Train Sort uses third-party services for advertising, in-app purchases, game functionality, analytics, security, and performance. These services may automatically collect or process certain information as described below.

2. Information Collected or Processed

Depending on the services used and the user's device, information collected or processed by Train Sort and its service providers may include:

  • Device and advertising identifiers, including identifiers used to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising and related services.
  • Approximate location information, which may be inferred from information such as an IP address. Train Sort does not request precise GPS location for gameplay.
  • Purchase history and transaction information relating to purchases made through Google Play, such as the purchase of the Remove Ads feature.
  • App activity and advertising interactions, such as interactions with advertisements and related events.
  • Diagnostic and technical information, such as device, application, network, and performance information used to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve services.

We do not use Train Sort to request your precise location, contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, health information, text messages, or financial account or payment-card details.

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected or processed through Train Sort may be used for:

  • Providing and operating game and purchase functionality;
  • Displaying, delivering, and measuring advertisements;
  • Analytics and service measurement;
  • Diagnosing technical problems and improving performance;
  • Fraud prevention, security, and regulatory compliance;
  • Processing and validating in-app purchases.

4. Advertising

Train Sort is supported by advertising. The Game uses Unity advertising and mediation technologies, including Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads.

These advertising services may collect or process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising interactions, purchase-related information, and technical or diagnostic information for purposes including advertising, analytics, measurement, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Depending on your region and applicable law, consent or privacy choices may be presented regarding advertising and related data processing.

5. In-App Purchases

Train Sort may offer optional in-app purchases, including a Remove Ads purchase. Purchases are processed through Google Play and Unity In-App Purchasing technology.

Mastazee Studios does not directly receive or store your full payment-card or bank-account details. Payment processing is handled by the applicable app store and payment services.

Purchase and transaction information may be processed to provide purchased features, validate transactions, support analytics, and operate related services.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

Train Sort uses third-party services that may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. These include:

  • Unity Technologies – including Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and Unity In-App Purchasing.
  • Google – including Google Play and related Android and purchase services.
  • Advertising partners made available through the advertising mediation services used by Train Sort.

The advertising partners available through mediation may change over time as the advertising configuration of the Game is updated.

You can learn more about these providers' privacy practices at:

  • Unity Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Security

We use service providers that employ security measures designed to protect information transmitted by their services. Data transmitted by the services used in Train Sort is transmitted using secure connections where supported and required.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Train Sort does not require a Mastazee Studios user account and Mastazee Studios does not maintain a separate online player-account database for the Game.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the applicable provider's retention policies, legal obligations, and legitimate operational requirements.

Users may contact Mastazee Studios with privacy or data-related questions or requests using the email address below. Where information is controlled by a third-party provider, requests may need to be directed to that provider in accordance with its privacy procedures.

9. Children's Privacy

Train Sort is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly ask users to provide personal information directly to Mastazee Studios through the Game. Advertising and other third-party services are configured and operated subject to applicable laws, platform requirements, and provider policies.

10. International Processing

Third-party service providers used by Train Sort may process information in countries other than the country in which you live. Their handling of information is governed by their applicable privacy policies and legal obligations.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Train Sort, its service providers, or applicable legal requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed accordingly.
